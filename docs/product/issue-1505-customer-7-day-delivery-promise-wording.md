# Issue 1505 — Customer 7-Day Delivery Promise Wording

## Purpose

This document defines customer-safe commercial wording for the Sekhem 7-day promise.

The promise means production-track activation readiness within 7 days after commercial commitment. It does not mean full Phase 1 delivery is complete in 7 days.

Full Phase 1 delivery remains a controlled 10–12 week implementation after contract signing.

## 1. Short Executive Version

Sekhem can be prepared for production-track activation readiness within 7 days after commercial commitment. This gives the customer a controlled starting point for onboarding, workspace preparation, stakeholder alignment, and Phase 1 delivery planning.

Full Phase 1 delivery is then completed over 10–12 weeks after contract signing.

## 2. Sales-Call Version

The 7-day commitment is a fast activation start, not a claim that the full platform is finished in one week.

Within 7 days, Sekhem can prepare the customer workspace, align stakeholders, confirm the main operating flow, and begin the controlled onboarding path.

The full Phase 1 delivery includes the Core Platform, R&D Radar, AI CTO / Repo Intelligence, Release Factory, and Security Gate. That full delivery is planned over 10–12 weeks after contract signing.

## 3. Proposal / Deck Version

### Sekhem Phase 1 Commercial Activation

Sekhem is positioned at launch-commercial stage: ready for controlled customer activation, commercial onboarding, and Phase 1 delivery planning.

### 7-Day Production-Track Activation Readiness

Within 7 days after commercial commitment, Sekhem can prepare the customer for production-track activation readiness.

This includes:

- customer workspace activation preparation
- stakeholder and access alignment
- Phase 1 delivery kickoff structure
- product journey walkthrough
- activation readiness review
- implementation planning for the first Phase 1 modules
- controlled onboarding path for the customer team

This does not mean full Phase 1 delivery is complete in 7 days.

### Full Phase 1 Delivery Timeline

Full Phase 1 activation and delivery is planned over 10–12 weeks after contract signing.

Phase 1 includes:

- Core Platform
- R&D Radar
- AI CTO / Repo Intelligence
- Release Factory / Security Gate

## 4. Customer-Facing Meaning

The customer should understand that Sekhem is ready to begin a serious commercial activation path quickly, while the full Phase 1 implementation remains a controlled 10–12 week project.

The 7-day promise covers readiness, onboarding, environment preparation, commercial activation start, and alignment.

It is not a claim that the full platform is deployed, customized, integrated, accepted, and customer-production-proven in one week.

## 5. What Is Included in 7-Day Readiness

The 7-day readiness window may include:

- activation kickoff
- customer workspace preparation
- access and stakeholder alignment
- product journey walkthrough
- Phase 1 implementation planning
- readiness review for the selected environment
- initial module activation planning
- delivery governance setup
- first customer-facing operating model walkthrough

## 6. What Remains in the 10–12 Week Phase 1 Delivery

The following remain part of the 10–12 week Phase 1 delivery:

- full Phase 1 platform delivery
- final environment hardening
- module activation completion
- customer-specific configuration
- implementation validation
- user onboarding and role setup
- acceptance testing
- final handover
- customer acceptance sign-off
- Phase 1 completion

## 8. Forbidden Claims List

Do not claim:

- full Phase 1 is complete in 7 days
- production is live in 7 days
- AI approves decisions
- AI deploys
- AI creates tasks automatically
- AI writes to GitHub
- GitHub write actions are active
- candidate-specific EvidenceReview filtering exists
- scheduled or public automation workflows are complete
- advanced modules are fully active
- production release automation is complete
- previous production customers exist for the full complete version

Use instead:

- production-track activation readiness
- controlled activation path
- commercial onboarding
- Phase 1 delivery over 10–12 weeks
- human approval required
- read-only readiness unless separately activated
- advanced modules are controlled roadmap or locked until validated

## Final Safe Positioning

Sekhem is at launch-commercial stage.

The commercial promise is fast activation readiness within 7 days, followed by full Phase 1 delivery over 10–12 weeks after contract signing.

This protects customer expectations and avoids overstating the current product state.
